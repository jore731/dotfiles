## Search Tips

- **Most content in BASANTCLOUD is in Dutch** — search with both Dutch and English terms
- Common Dutch terms: `kosten` (costs), `wijzigen` (change), `opzetten` (set up), `aanvragen` (request), `verbinding` (connection), `toegang` (access), `verwijderen` (delete)
- **Labels** are useful for filtering: `application-owner`, `devops`, `glossary`, `adf`, `azure-sql-server`, `service-principal`
- **S4Y articles** follow the pattern `GS0000XXX` or `GSP000XXX`
