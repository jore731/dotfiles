# Customer Co-creation — Reference Materials

## Session Design Template

Customer Co-creation runs across **multiple sessions** (typically 3–5 over 4–12 weeks). Each session builds on the previous one.

| Session | Focus | Duration | Output |
|---------|-------|----------|--------|
| **Session 1** | Inspiration & challenge framing — shared understanding of the problem | 3–4 hrs | Agreed problem statement; customer pain map |
| **Session 2** | Divergent ideation — many rough concepts generated together | 3–4 hrs | 10–20 rough concept sketches per team |
| **Session 3** | Convergence & prototyping — down-select and build low-fidelity prototypes | Full day | 3–5 co-created prototypes |
| **Session 4** | Validation — test prototypes with real users or customers outside the room | 2–3 hrs + field time | Customer feedback, ranked prototypes |
| **Session 5 (optional)** | Refinement & handover — finalise concept; align on next steps | 2–3 hrs | Concept brief ready for BSD or PoC |

---

## Participant Selection Criteria

| Criterion | Guidance |
|-----------|---------|
| Engagement | Select customers who are invested in the problem — not necessarily the largest accounts |
| Diversity | Mix segments, geographies, or use cases to avoid single-perspective bias |
| Confidentiality | All participants must sign an NDA before Session 1 if pre-commercial concepts will be shared |
| Continuity | Ideally the same participants across all sessions (reduces re-briefing; deepens trust) |
| Size | 10–30 participants total; 4–6 per working group within each session |

---

## Session Facilitation Principles

- **Co-design, not feedback:** Customers build things, they don't just react to what BASF presents
- **Equal voice:** Structured techniques (e.g., silent brainstorming, dot voting) prevent dominant voices
- **Show, don't tell:** Use prototypes, not slide decks, to trigger concrete reactions
- **Suspend judgement during ideation:** All ideas are valid in the build phase
- **Document everything:** Assign a documentarian; photograph all artefacts at the end of each session

---

## Close-the-Loop Requirement

After the final session, communicate to all participants:

1. Which concepts were selected to move forward — and why
2. What the next step is (BSD, PoC, full development)
3. What happened to the concepts that were not selected — archived with learnings, or referred elsewhere

> Customers who co-create and then hear nothing will not participate again. The follow-up is not optional.

---

## Materials Needed per Session

**Session 1 (Framing):**
- Customer journey map template (printed A1)
- Pain-point cards for prioritisation
- Dot voting stickers

**Sessions 2–3 (Ideation + Prototyping):**
- Concept canvas template (problem / solution / who benefits / key unknowns)
- Prototyping materials (→ see `references/jamming.md` for materials list)
- Camera for documentation

**Session 4 (Validation):**
- Prototype testing guide (observation guide → see `references/empathic-design.md`)
- Feedback capture sheet per prototype
- Ranking grid (evaluation criteria agreed in Session 1)
