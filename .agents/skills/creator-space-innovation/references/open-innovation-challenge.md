# Open Innovation Challenge — Reference Materials

## Platform Selection Criteria

When selecting the web campaign platform, evaluate against these criteria:

| Criterion | What to check |
|-----------|--------------|
| **Reach** | Does the platform's solver community match the target expertise? (e.g., materials science, biotech, logistics) |
| **IP protection** | Who owns submitted IP? Read platform T&Cs carefully before signing. |
| **Moderation tools** | Can you filter, approve, and respond to submissions? |
| **Analytics** | Can you track views, registrations, and submissions over time? |
| **Cost structure** | Setup fee + success fee (some platforms charge a % of prize pool) |
| **Submission format** | Does the platform support the format you need? (text, video, technical documents) |
| **Integration** | Can submissions be exported to internal evaluation tools? |
| **Brand alignment** | Can you white-label or customise the challenge page with BASF branding? |

**Common platforms:** NineSigma, InnoCentive, Wazoku, HeroX, BASF Inno-360

## Challenge Description Template

The challenge description is the most critical document. It determines whether the solver community understands the problem well enough to submit relevant solutions.

```
CHALLENGE TITLE
A compelling, plain-language title (not internal project code)

THE PROBLEM
What is happening today that is not acceptable?
Who is affected and how?
What have existing approaches tried — and why have they fallen short?
(2–4 paragraphs; no internal jargon)

WHAT WE ARE LOOKING FOR
Describe the ideal solution in terms of OUTCOMES, not technologies.
What performance level must the solution achieve?
What constraints must it respect? (e.g., regulatory, cost, environmental)
What constraints can be challenged?
(Bullet list, max 8 items)

WHAT WE ARE NOT LOOKING FOR
List 2–4 explicit exclusions to prevent off-topic submissions.

EVALUATION CRITERIA
How will solutions be judged? (list criteria + relative weighting)

PRIZE / COLLABORATION OPPORTUNITY
What does the winner receive?
Is this a cash prize, a collaboration agreement, or both?
What is the timeline from submission to award?

ELIGIBILITY
Who can submit? (individuals, teams, companies, geographic restrictions)
Any restrictions on participants? (competitors, current BASF employees)

TIMELINE
Challenge opens: [date]
Submission deadline: [date]
Shortlist notification: [date]
Award decision: [date]
```

## Evaluation Process

### Phase 1: Eligibility Screening (week 1–2 after close)
- Verify all submissions meet eligibility criteria
- Remove duplicates and incomplete submissions
- Result: shortlist of eligible submissions

### Phase 2: Technical Review (week 2–4)
- Internal subject-matter experts score each eligible submission against technical criteria
- Target: reduce to top 10–20% of submissions
- Result: ranked technical shortlist

### Phase 3: Expert Jury Evaluation (week 4–6)
- External and internal jury reviews technical shortlist
- Scores against full evaluation criteria (technical + strategic + commercial)
- Deliberation and consensus meeting
- Result: ranked finalist list (typically 3–10)

### Phase 4: Deep Engagement with Finalists (week 6–8)
- Q&A sessions with each finalist to clarify proposal
- NDA signed if confidential BASF context will be shared
- Final ranking and award decision

## IP and Legal Checklist

- [ ] Legal counsel reviewed platform T&Cs for IP ownership clauses
- [ ] Submission form includes explicit IP declaration (solver confirms they own the IP)
- [ ] NDA prepared for finalist-stage deep engagement
- [ ] Prize structure and award conditions reviewed by legal/procurement
- [ ] If collaboration agreement is the prize: template contract drafted before launch
- [ ] Data protection / GDPR compliance for solver registration data confirmed

---

## Challenge Description Quality Checklist

- [ ] Problem described from the perspective of those affected — not BASF's internal vocabulary
- [ ] Desired outcome is described in terms of performance (what the solution must achieve) not technology (what it must use)
- [ ] Explicit exclusions prevent off-topic submissions wasting reviewer time
- [ ] Evaluation criteria are published and specific enough for solvers to self-assess relevance
- [ ] Challenge tested with 2–3 people outside the team before publication
- [ ] No confidential BASF information in the public challenge description

## Post-Competition Plan

After the award, the challenge should continue to generate value:

| Action | Timing | Owner |
|--------|--------|-------|
| Announce winner publicly (with consent) | Award day | Communications lead |
| Share results summary with all participants | Within 1 week | Programme manager |
| Initiate collaboration agreement with winner | Within 2 weeks | Legal + business owner |
| PoC milestone plan agreed with winner | Within 4 weeks | Technical lead |
| Capture lessons learned from the challenge process | Within 2 weeks | Programme manager |
| Decide whether to run follow-on challenge | Within 3 months | Sponsor |
| Track and communicate PoC outcomes | Ongoing | Business owner |
